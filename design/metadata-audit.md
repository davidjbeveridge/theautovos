# Site metadata audit — October 21, 2026

Scope: all 46 main-site registry pages and the real 404, across production, hosted owner preview and local simulation. The separate Squarespace merchandise catalog keeps its platform-managed metadata; prototype/catalog examples remain excluded from indexing. This audit does not alter commerce or DNS.

| Finding | Impact | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Porsche tint, Targa tint and Grenadier sharing defaults used 320×180 stills. The complete local `verify-site.mjs` release check still enforces the private Google-document Q&A sync receipt. Public CI runs the metadata-specific artifact audit, which needs no private document access; the unchanged full release verifier passes locally.

Retained deliberately: verified video upload dates and durations; truthful FAQ ownership; no fabricated aggregate ratings, offers, prices, geocoordinates, personal authors, article publication dates, X handles or Facebook app IDs. FAQ markup describes visible answers; it is not a promise of a Google rich result. The 404 is excluded from indexing/sitemaps, production pages are indexable, and owner previews remain noindex.

Verification: canonical/source builds and verifiers; 35 unit tests; 141 complete metadata/graph/package checks (47 pages × three modes); Jacques fast and registry checks; 189 local Worker checks with an explicit loopback-origin fixture; 50 rendered Chrome metadata/brand-journey checks, including actual image decoding and dimensions. All nine article bylines are checked at 390/768/1440px with normal-text contrast on dark headers, and representative mobile/desktop headers are visually inspected. Q&A remains 135 answers with its existing digest hash. External map, video and spam-provider requests are blocked during local Chrome proof; no live inquiry is submitted.

Release: commit, passing PR/main CI, merge, then deploy matching main to the existing production and owner-preview Workers. Compare every hosted page and sharing image with the built artifact, check crawler user agents and discovery files, and record the actual versions and results in `design-artifacts/social-metadata-audit/`. These checks do not claim a Google Rich Results Test pass, Search Console coverage, live rankings or a social platform's cached preview refresh.
