03 / Image

Real studio evidence. Intentional crops.
Show the real people, cars and work.

Small moves. Clear feedback.

Photos and cards settle into view over 420ms. A pointer hover gives images a 1.02× zoom; actions and lightboxes respond over 180ms. Touch works without hover. Reduced motion removes movement, and every image stays discoverable without JavaScript.

Voice with a purpose.

Explain what it does, what it doesn’t do, and what comes next. No invented guarantees, deadlines, prices or customer stories.

05

Make it feel like Auto Vos.

Practical rules for creating the next page and keeping the whole experience consistent.

Start with the visitor.

Give each page one clear job: explain a service, show the work, introduce the studio or help someone take the next step. Choose the closest page example before making a new layout.

Compose in a clear order.

Lead with a direct headline. Answer the main question. Show relevant work. Resolve likely concerns. Finish with one clear invitation. Remove sections that do not help that journey.

Choose the right action.

Use a solid button for the main action, an outlined button for a secondary choice and a text link for supporting navigation. Name the outcome: “Get a quote,” “View our work” or “Choose a size.”

Give the work room.

Use large, purposeful photographs and short descriptions. Align related content. Follow the spacing scale; separate chapters with space or a fine line. Keep decoration out of the way.

Design every state.

Show what is selected, what is unavailable and what happens next. For errors, explain the problem and keep entered details. For empty collections, give a useful reset or next step.

Keep it useful on a phone.

Stack columns in reading order, let headings wrap and make actions easy to tap. Keep labels and essential details visible. Check narrow screens, enlarged text and keyboard navigation.

One reference for every page.

This catalog is the canonical design reference for the rebuild. Reuse these colors, type styles, spacing, components and page patterns. Add a new variation only when an existing pattern cannot serve the visitor’s task.

Review the whole journey.

Follow the path from homepage to service, work and quote. Check that every link has a destination, every action has feedback and each page makes its next step clear.
